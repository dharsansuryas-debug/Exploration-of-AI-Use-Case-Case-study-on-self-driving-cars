# Exploration-of-AI-Use-Case-Case-study-on-self-driving-cars
# AIM
To study the application of Artificial Intelligence (AI) in self-driving cars and understand how AI technologies help vehicles perceive their surroundings, make decisions, and navigate safely without continuous human control.

# THEORY
Self-driving cars, also known as autonomous vehicles, are vehicles that use Artificial Intelligence (AI) to navigate roads and perform driving tasks with reduced human intervention. Examples include vehicles developed by Waymo and Tesla.

These vehicles use the following AI technologies:
1. Computer Vision: Identifies road signs, traffic lights, pedestrians, vehicles, and lane markings using cameras.
2. Machine Learning (ML): Helps the vehicle recognize patterns and improve object detection and prediction.
3. Sensor Fusion: Combines data from cameras, radar, and other sensors to understand the surroundings.
4. Path Planning: Determines a suitable route and driving path while considering obstacles and traffic conditions.
5. Decision-Making Algorithms: Help the vehicle decide when to stop, accelerate, brake, or change lanes.
## WORKING PRINCIPLE

1. Data Collection: Sensors such as cameras, radar, and LiDAR collect information about the vehicle's surroundings.
2. Perception: AI and computer vision identify vehicles, pedestrians, traffic signs, road markings, and obstacles.
3. Localization: The vehicle determines its current position using GPS, maps, and other sensors.
4. Path Planning: AI calculates a suitable path based on road conditions, traffic, and obstacles.
5. Decision Making: The system decides whether to accelerate, brake, stop, or turn.
6. Vehicle Control: Electronic control systems operate the steering, brakes, and accelerator according to the planned actions.
7. Continuous Monitoring: The vehicle continuously collects new sensor data and updates its decisions while moving.

Example: When a pedestrian crosses the road, the self-driving car detects the pedestrian, predicts the possible movement, and applies the brakes if necessary to avoid a collision.

# APPLICATION
- Autonomous transportation and robotaxis.
- Traffic monitoring and route optimization.
- Driver assistance and collision avoidance.
- Improved mobility for people who cannot drive.
- Efficient transportation and logistics.

# RESULT
The application of Artificial Intelligence in self-driving cars was studied successfully. The roles of computer vision, machine learning, sensor fusion, and path planning in detecting surroundings, making driving decisions, and navigating roads were understood. AI can improve transportation efficiency and safety, but autonomous vehicles still face challenges such as unpredictable road conditions, sensor limitations, and complex traffic situations. Human supervision and appropriate safety measures remain important, depending on the vehicle's automation level.
